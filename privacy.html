Privacy Policy

Lookalike for Mac · Last updated 22 October 2026

Short version: your photos never leave your Mac. Lookalike collects no data, has no account, no analytics and no tracking.

Your photos

With your permission, Lookalike reads photos from your Photos library or from folders you choose. It analyses them on your Mac to find duplicates, similar shots and blurry photos. The photos, and everything worked out from them, stay on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to anyone else.

Photos from iCloud Photos may be downloaded in preview size by macOS itself so they can be compared. That happens between your Mac and Apple, as it does for the Photos app.

Removing photos

Lookalike only removes photos you selected and confirmed. Library photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, and folder photos go to the Trash, so both can be restored.

What Lookalike stores

A count of how many photos you removed with the free version, kept in Lookalike's own settings on your Mac. Nothing else.

Purchases

Lookalike Pro is bought through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the payment. The developer receives no payment details or personal information from Apple.

Children

Lookalike collects no data from anyone, including children.
